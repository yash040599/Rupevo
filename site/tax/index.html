Tax guides on Rupevo are general information for Indian residents, written for returns for FY 2025-26 (filed in 2026) under the Income-tax Act, 1961. They are not tax advice; verify with a chartered accountant before filing. The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from tax year 2026-27 and the guides will be updated for it.
