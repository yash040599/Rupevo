Fidelity · NASDAQ: MSFT

Microsoft shares at Fidelity

Your Indian income-tax return for Microsoft RSUs and ESPP shares held at Fidelity. Load your Fidelity exports once and get, at SBI's TT buying rates, the Schedule FA rows to type in, the capital gains on shares you sold, and your dividend income with the US tax credit.

Foreign assets: Schedule FA, AL Selling shares: CG, FSI Dividends: OS, FSI, TR, Form 67

1Export your shares from Fidelity

  1. Sign in to NetBenefits and open your Stock Plan Account.
  2. Under Owned assets, find MICROSOFT CORP and click View share details, just below the quantity.
  3. In the window that opens, stay on Current shares and choose Asset currency, so values are in US dollars rather than converted to rupees.
  4. Click Export at the top right. Your browser saves View open lots.csv.
  5. Sold or transferred any shares? Click Previously held shares in the same window and Export again: your browser saves View closed lots.csv.

View open lots.csv lists the shares you still hold. View closed lots.csv lists the ones you sold or transferred: it gives the capital gains, and Schedule FA and the dividends need it too, because those shares are still reported for the year you sold them and were paid dividends until the sale. Never sold or transferred any shares? Then View open lots.csv alone is enough. Keep Asset currency for both.

2Load the files

📄Load both files for complete results: shares you sold change Schedule FA and the dividends too, not only the capital gains. Never sold or transferred any shares? View open lots.csv alone is enough.

🔒Your files are read on your own device. They are not uploaded, sent anywhere or saved; closing the page clears them.

📱Works on phones too: tap Choose files and pick the exports from Files or Downloads, together or one at a time. Exporting it from Fidelity is easiest on a laptop, on the NetBenefits website.
