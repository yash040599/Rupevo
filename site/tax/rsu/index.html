RSU taxation

For Indian residents who hold RSUs (restricted stock units) of a foreign employer. Choose your broker and company, and the guide walks you through what your return needs: vesting, dividends, sales and the foreign-asset disclosure.

What the guide covers

📈 Vesting

Shares that vest are taxed as salary (a perquisite): your employer withholds the tax and the value appears in your Form 16. That value is also your cost when you sell.

💵 Dividends

Taxed in India at your slab rate. The US usually withholds 25% first (with a W-8BEN on file); you can claim credit for it through Form 67 and Schedule TR.

🔁 Selling

A capital gain or loss: long-term if you held the shares for more than 24 months (12.5%, no indexation), otherwise short-term at your slab rate.

🌍 Foreign assets (Schedule FA)

List every foreign holding, the shares and the brokerage account, for the calendar year (January–December 2025 for FY 2025-26), even if you sold nothing. This needs ITR-2 or ITR-3, and leaving it out can attract penalties under the Black Money Act.
